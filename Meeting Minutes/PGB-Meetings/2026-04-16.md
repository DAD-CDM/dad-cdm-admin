* **Project Updates and Deadlines Review**  
  * The team discussed several ongoing projects and upcoming deadlines.They reviewed conference submissions, including TrustCon and [disinfo.EU](http://disinfo.EU).

* **DAD-CDM Presentation Consolidation Progress**  
  * Danielle presented her work on consolidating a presentation. She shared her progress on developing a "DAD-CDM in a Nutshell" concept that includes threat landscape information, information manipulation themes, and real-world impact examples. Danielle also outlined potential blog content ideas, including modeling the Operation Stolen Valor use case and creating content about AI-generated content with agentic distribution, and requested team members to add their social media handles to a spreadsheet for content amplification and provide recommendations for the PGB prospects list.  
* **TSC Update**  
  * Margot provided an update on the TSC work in 2026, focusing on updating holistic objects with three main principles: backward compatibility, flexibility and simplicity, and using existing resources. They have been working on various entities including selectors, channels, narratives, incidents, and impact objects, while also developing definitions and taxonomies to ensure harmony with existing standards. Margot mentioned that the TSC is finalizing two documents \- one on threat actors and related objects, and another on modeling channels with four different approaches. The team aims to complete these documents by the end of April, with the current focus on simplifying the detailed proposals for easier stakeholder understanding.  
* **TSC Timeline and Communications Planning**  
  * The group discussed their timeline for finalizing documents and external communications related to the TSC. They plan to use April to complete internal discussions, May for external communication efforts including GitHub and social media, and June to discuss remaining objects and provide content for external communication. Margot mentioned that Anais and Alberto have joined the TSC.  
* **Project Restructuring and Management Updates**  
  * The team discussed project restructuring, with Margot explaining that she has taken on more organizational management responsibilities to track projects and meet deadlines while other team members focus on content development. Margot shared a project tracker document with the team for review.  
* **Sponsorship Planning**  
  * Max discussed the need to socialize the project and secure more sponsors, emphasizing the importance of having an estimated timeline for when V1 will be ready for release. Margot explained that the main objects should be finalized for feedback by the end of June, but the timeline for when these updates will be available to everyone depends on the OASIS timeline, which is unclear. Max suggested setting an aspirational date to help communicate progress to potential stakeholders, and the group agreed this would be helpful for future discussions.  
* **STIX Version Targeting Milestone Discussion**

They agreed to leverage Stephen's contribution on incident and event tracking to create a blog post highlighting their impact on the standard. Danielle committed to reaching out to Stephen for updates on the timeline and potentially collaborating on the blog post.
